Procedural Geometry Gallery

Nine mathematical surfaces, fractals and particle systems, each animated per frame. A single WebGL renderer draws all of them, scissoring one viewport per card, so the page costs one context instead of nine.

9 scenes 1 renderer ~2,400 morphed vertices in the noise blob 2,000 orbiting particles Offscreen cards skip rendering
WebGL isn't available in this browser, so the nine scenes can't be drawn. The captions below still describe each one.
1.1

Torus Knot Loop

A continuous (2,3) knot woven around an invisible torus, so the tube never intersects itself however far it winds.

TorusKnotGeometry(1, 0.35, 160, 24) · wireframe

1.2

Möbius Strip

A ribbon given a half twist before its ends are joined, leaving it with one continuous side and one edge.

BufferGeometry · 48 × 24 parametric grid · DoubleSide

1.3

Superquadric Morph

One radial equation swept through its exponent: below 2 it pinches into a star, at exactly 2 it is a sphere, above it swells toward a cube.

|x|ᵉ + |y|ᵉ + |z|ᵉ = 1 · e swept 0.7 → 5.0

2.1

Polyhedra Cluster

A wireframe dodecahedron turning around a solid octahedron on its own axis, the two rotations deliberately out of phase.

DodecahedronGeometry(1.4) + OctahedronGeometry(0.78)

2.2

Geodesic Buckyball

An icosahedron subdivided twice and pushed back onto the sphere — the triangulation behind geodesic domes and C₆₀.

IcosahedronGeometry(1.5, detail 2) · 320 faces

2.3

Sierpiński Tetrahedron

Each tetrahedron replaced by four half-scale copies, three levels deep. Volume falls to zero while surface area holds.

Recursive build · depth 3 · 64 tetrahedra

3.1

Organic Noise Blob

A sphere whose every vertex is pushed along its own normal by 3D gradient noise, re-evaluated and re-shaded each frame.

SphereGeometry(1.2, 48, 48) · Perlin 3D · MeshStandardMaterial

3.2

Harmonic Spike Sphere

Spherical harmonics drive the displacement, so the spikes sit in a standing-wave pattern that breathes rather than flickers.

r = 1.2 + 0.45 · sin(6θ) · sin(5φ) · pulse(t)

3.3

Particle Swarm Core

Two thousand points on independent orbits, each with its own radius, inclination and speed, breathing in and out of the core.

Points · 2,000 vertices · additive blending
