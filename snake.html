NEON SNAKE

The field is four screens wide — the view follows you, and the minimap in the corner shows where the food is.
Clear every piece on the board to reach the next level.
Walls don't kill you: you glance off them and keep going. Your own tail and the grey blocks still bite.

Rival snakes are loose on the same field, hunting the same food. Run into one and you die; cut one off and it dies instead.

◀ ▶ turn you left and right from wherever you're heading, so you never need a setup move. ▲ ▼, arrow keys, WASD and swipes all work too.
