01Code quality

Separate pieces sounded like separate voices
Fixed

Since review 82, an answer's first sentence went alone, then up to 160 characters a piece, and each piece was its own Gemini recording. Those can differ in pitch and delivery, so one answer could sound like two speakers. Streaming makes the split unnecessary: a long line starts as fast as a short one. VOICE_LIVE is now 600, the proxy's limit, and the first-sentence rule is gone. An answer over 600 characters splits at whole sentences into as few pieces as fit. That is two for the suite's 12-sentence answer.

Broken copy: pieces back to 160 characters (failed 1 check).
His voice switched to the browser's partway through
Fixed

The "all or nothing" rule for his voice applied only when an answer was planned. A piece that failed while he spoke was said by the browser in place. Now, in his voice: if a piece fails before he has said anything, the rest of the answer is said in the browser's voice in one go. If a piece fails after he has begun, it is asked for once more, and if it fails again he stops there. His tag then ends STOPPED · GEMINI VOICE FAILED in amber, and the chat still shows the whole answer. "Mixed" keeps the piece-by-piece fallback it is meant to have. The suite fails the proxy at /api/voice itself (a Google failure on the stream alone is recovered by the proxy's own fallback, which the first version of this check didn't account for). It covers a retry that works, a retry that fails, a one-piece answer failing first, and a two-piece answer failing first. ownVoice: 152 checks, up from 144. Chromium step 13: 23 checks, no page errors, no CSP violations.

Broken copies (each failed): his voice falling back mid-answer like mixed (4 checks); no second try (2); a stop not tagged (1); a first failure saying only that piece in the browser's voice. That last one first passed, because the check used a one-piece answer, where "that piece" and "the rest" are the same. A two-piece check was added, and the copy then failed it (1).
