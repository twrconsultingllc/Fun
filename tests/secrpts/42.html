A held fist can't flood the frame
Confirmed clean

02Code quality & accessibility

The network logic is tested without WebGL
Confirmed clean

The suite derives what it expects instead of reading it off the page. For example, 4-6-6-3 has 4·6 + 6·6 + 6·3 = 78 connections, layers are 9 apart (x = −13.5, −4.5, 4.5, 13.5), and the tallest layer's half-height is (6−1)/2 · 3.2 = 8. With a seeded random number generator, it checks that a burst reaches every hidden layer and the output within 3 s, and that no pulse ever arrives back at the input. Pulses and glow die out once bursts stop. An answer lights exactly the output layer, and a squeeze lights all 19 nodes and sends 78 pulses. It also checks six phrasings of the command, including "use your brain" and "brain teaser", which must not open it, and that the help answer mentions it.
