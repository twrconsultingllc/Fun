Review #34 — sentinel-web.html "Map this place"

Step 1 of the room-mapping feature. Map this place on the Nearby screen leads through four screens. Start point fixes where you stand and which way you face. Turn in a circle is a compass dial that uses your body blocking the signal behind you to give each device a direction. Walk the area counts your steps with the motion sensor and tracks your path by compass, recording every device along the way, and follows one device hot and cold. Results is a top-down plan with a hot zone for every device heard. Photos, waypoints, the device inventory and floor plans are steps 2 and 3. Also, "Find it" is now offered for every device, not only flagged ones.

01Security & privacy

"Find it" and mapping now work on any device, anywhere (the user's decision)
Open

Until now the hot/cold finder only worked on an active flag. That followed the Sentinel app's guardrail, which exists so the tool can find a tracker following you but can't be used to follow a chosen person's phone or earbuds. On 2026-09-27 the user decided on full mapping everywhere: every device can be mapped and found, flagged or not, at home or elsewhere. They were offered a middle option first (full mapping only at places marked as home). startFinder() no longer checks for a flag, and the suite now asserts "Find it" appears on an unflagged device. What still holds: the page only listens (no connections), keeps nothing from a survey after the page closes, and shows Chrome's per-site IDs, never real addresses. Recorded as open because it's a deliberate removal of a protection, not a fix.

Survey data stays in memory; no new network use
Confirmed clean

The sweep, walked path, step count and per-device readings live only in the page's memory and are gone when it closes. Nothing is written to IndexedDB yet (step 2 will store photos and waypoints, with delete). The CSP is unchanged from 32.html (diff checked), and a grep for fetch(, localStorage, innerHTML, insertAdjacentHTML, document.write and eval finds nothing. The compass (deviceorientationabsolute) and motion (devicemotion) listeners are added only while a survey is open and removed when you leave it (asserted). Where a browser requires a permission for them, it's requested first.

Names still rendered only as text
Confirmed clean

Device names in the results list use textContent through el(). On the plan canvas they're drawn with fillText, cut to 24 characters. No new HTML-building path was added.

02Code quality & accessibility

Sweep direction was biased and missed the last sector
Fixed

Two real bugs, found when the scripted survey's direction came out at 108° for a device strongest at 90°. First, the sweep ended the moment its twelfth sector was touched, so that sector never got a reading, which skewed the weighted mean. Second, each sector counted at its centre angle, adding up to 15° of bias. The sweep now finishes after at least 345° of actual turning with every sector covered, and uses the headings actually faced in each sector. In a headless Chromium simulation of a room (known device positions, signal from distance, body shadowing and ±2 dB noise), the directions came out 5–8° from the true bearings.

Fix: circular mean of the actual headings per sector, and a total-turn finish. The first version, "finish on re-entering the first sector", never fired when compass smoothing left the heading at 358°, so it was replaced by counting the turn.
"Start here" stayed disabled, and the dial's text went stale
Fixed

Found in Chromium. A compass reading didn't trigger a re-render, so "Start here" stayed disabled until something else redrew the screen. And the new once-a-second limit on soft re-renders dropped updates without catching up, so the dial read "1 of 12 covered, facing N" after a turn to 250°. Compass readings, sweep progress and steps now request a render, and a skipped render always runs a moment later. Re-checked: "9 of 12 directions covered · facing W" after turning from 20° to 250°.

Fix: scheduleRender() on sensor input, plus a catch-up redraw on throttled renders.
Busy screens are rebuilt at most once a second
Fixed

Nearby and the survey used to rebuild their buttons on every advertisement. With many devices that can be several times a second, which risks replacing a button between finger-down and finger-up, so the tap is lost. Soft re-renders of those two screens are now limited to once a second, with a catch-up render. The swarm and plan canvases keep animating at full rate, because they're not rebuilt.

How accurate the hot zones are
Partial

In the Chromium room simulation, a device the walk went around was placed 0.7 m from its true spot. Two devices outside the walked loop were placed 2.1 m and 2.9 m off, at the edge of the path, because a power-weighted centre can't go past the places you stood. The walk screen now says to walk around a device, not just past it, and the results call positions "this part of the room". Distance from signal strength is a rough indoor path-loss estimate (1 m at −59 dBm). Real walls, bodies and antennas will make it rougher. Marked partial because the limit is inherent and disclosed, not removed.

Tests and accessibility
Confirmed clean

The sentinel-web suite went from 89 to 122 checks, all passing. They cover heading maths, path-loss distances, the sweep (east points east within one sector, flat is "unclear", two sectors give nothing), step counting (a 2 Hz walk for 5 s gives about 10 steps, standing still gives none), dead reckoning, both kinds of hot zone, and all four screens driven with simulated headings, steps and readings. Breaking the sweep's heading mean and raising the step threshold turned 2 checks red. No new colours were added: the dial and plan reuse the swarm's measured palette from 33.html. Both canvases are role="img" with a label that updates (directions covered, or steps and devices mapped), and every result is also in the text list below them with its offset from the start.

Not yet tried with real sensors
Open

Still to be seen on the tablet: that Chrome's deviceorientationabsolute reading converts to a compass heading as 360 − alpha on this device, how much furniture and wiring upset the compass, whether the step detector (1.3 m/s² above a running average, at least 300 ms apart) matches a real walking pace, and the 0.7 m step length. Left open until the user has walked a room with it.
