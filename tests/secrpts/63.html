Settings could carry an extra field until the next load
Fixed

Found by trying changes against the suite. store('jarvis-settings', …) checked only that the text wasn't personal, so a bug that put an extra field (a level, say) into the settings object would have saved it; cleanSettings() only dropped it on the next load. fits() now refuses a settings record holding anything cleanSettings() wouldn't keep (settingsOnly()), so it's checked inside save() like every other record. Pinned: {"color":"blue","level":3}, an unknown colour and an array are refused; the three real choices together are accepted.

A restored or hand-edited backup can't push a level past what fits() allows
Confirmed clean

Levels and favourites read the counts through fits() again (usageOf()), and ignore days after today. A hostile backup with 80 days of an unlisted ID, 80 future days, fractional counts, days as strings, four-field records, a meta key and a level key had all 190 bad days refused and both keys skipped, and left the level at 1, even though its totals (allowed: a listed ID and a whole number) marked everything found. Totals are visits, not days, so they never add days. Seventy future days written straight into the database also left level 1. A valid backup with 60 real days in the kept window did raise the level, which is what fits() allows.

A level can drop after a long time away
Open

By design: a level is worked out from the 90 days kept by day plus the streak's carry. After about three months away, the days fall out of the window and the level falls with them. Keeping the highest level reached would be a new stored value, which this session ruled out.
