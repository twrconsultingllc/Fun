01Security

What reaches the page
Confirmed clean

The proxy adds one header to a voiced line, X-Jarvis-Voice: model=…; style=on|off|words (plus the version from "compare voices"), and exposes only that header to the page. It never carries the words. A Google quota refusal on the streamed path now passes on Google's message, scrubbed of any key like every other error (a check sends a key-shaped string and confirms it's gone). On the page, everything is set with textContent, and the reason text is cut to 160 characters. jarvis.html reads neither and is unchanged.

Broken copies (each failed jarvis-cloud): the header not exposed (1 check); Google's words dropped from a streamed 429 (1). jarvis-cloud: 264 checks, up from 259.

02Code quality

A details line under each tag, and the real reason
Confirmed clean

Under each line's voice tag is a details line: the model, streamed or not, the style, how long until the sound began, and the length. For a recording it says "recording from this site", and for a saved line "saved on this device, no call to Google". When a line fails, the tag names the cause: GOOGLE QUOTA REACHED with Google's own message, TOO MANY AT ONCE for the proxy's 40-a-minute limit, REFUSED BY GOOGLE, UNREADABLE AUDIO, TOO SLOW (no answer in 25 seconds) or UNREACHABLE. When the live voice is resting, it names the last failure. "Compare voices" no longer says SUPERCHARGER OFF when the voice is actually resting. On screens under 700 px tall the details hide in the collapsed strip with the tag. In Chromium, the face kept its size at all three widths, and a screenshot at 390 px shows the details under his line.

Broken copies (each failed ownVoice): a quota refusal shown as "busy" (2 checks); the details never shown (2). ownVoice: 174 checks. Chromium steps 10 and 13: 86 checks, then 26 on re-running step 13.
