Review #25 — trick-or-treat-dash.html (new page)

A new single-file canvas game: Trick-or-Treat Dash. You run down a street jumping puddles, pumpkins, tombstones and a giant blow-up ghost, stay under bats, and collect candy. Easy mode has no game over and a 600 m finish line; Normal speeds up and gives 3 hearts. Lifetime candy unlocks 8 costumes and is saved in localStorage. The page is linked from index.html, and a new dash test suite covers it. Also fixed in 24.html: its missing </head> tag.

01Security

CSP and referrer meta tags present, no outside origins
Confirmed clean
The page carries the repo's standard policy with nothing widened: img-src 'self' (no data:), object-src 'none', base-uri 'none', form-action 'self', plus referrer strict-origin-when-cross-origin and a description. A grep for (src|href)="https?:, @import and off-origin url( finds nothing. All art is canvas shapes and emoji, and all sound is synthesised, so nothing is fetched. 'unsafe-inline' stays in script-src because the script is inline by design, like every other single-file page.
No markup injection path
Confirmed clean
A grep for innerHTML finds 0 uses. Every string that reaches the DOM (HUD, costume names, game-over text) goes through textContent, and every one of them comes from constants in the script or from numbers. A grep for fetch(, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, eval(, new Function, document.write, sessionStorage, indexedDB and on*=" attributes also finds nothing.
Saved progress is validated on read
Confirmed clean
The page's one piece of browser storage is the fun.trickOrTreatDash.v1 key, holding {best, lifetime, costume, muted}. The key only ever holds game progress. Both the read and the write are in try/catch, so private mode or blocked storage just means progress isn't kept. On load:
  • Counts must be integers between 0 and 10,000,000.
  • The costume must be a known id that's already unlocked by the saved total.
  • muted must be exactly true.
The suite loads 6 hand-made saves (a good save, a not-yet-unlocked costume, string/negative/fractional numbers, an HTML payload as the costume id, corrupt JSON, and a bare JSON string). Each one loads without an error and falls back to safe values. A real Chromium reload after ending a 130-candy run brought the total back and unlocked exactly Ghost, Cat and Witch.
Clickjacking (frame-ancestors) not enforceable
Open
Same as every GitHub Pages page: frame-ancestors is ignored in a meta CSP, and Pages can't send headers. It's architecturally blocked, and the risk is low because the page has no privileged action to trick anyone into.

02Correctness & fairness

Jump height depended on frame rate
Fixed
The first version updated velocity and then position with plain Euler steps, which loses about v·dt/2 of height per jump. The suite measured a 138.7 px peak against the closed-form 142.2 at 120 fps, and the loss doubles at 60 fps and doubles again at 30. On a slow device, the double jump over the 148 px ghost could have failed.
Fixed: the update is now the exact constant-gravity formula (y += v·dt − g·dt²/2). The suite pins a peak of v²/2g within 3 px and the same height at 30 and 144 fps within 4 px.
Title-screen crash after a Normal game over
Fixed
Choosing Easy on the title screen after losing a Normal run threw RangeError: Invalid count value: Infinity. The heart display repeated the empty-heart character Easy.hearts − hearts times, and Easy's heart count is Infinity.
Fixed: the HUD now branches on the selected mode's heart limit, and shows full hearts on the title screen. There's a regression test for the exact click sequence.
Every obstacle clearable, with a forgiving timing window
Fixed
The suite searches jump (and double-jump) timings with the page's own step() and confirms every obstacle kind can be cleared at both the slowest and fastest speed it spawns at. It also confirms the giant ghost can't be cleared with a single jump, that bats are cleared by staying down, and that jumping into a bat hurts. It then measures the width of each timing window. At first, Easy was the least forgiving mode: its pumpkin window was 264 ms and its puddle window 288 ms, because at a slow run you're only in the air for about 165 px.
Fixed: Easy trims 12 px off each side of every obstacle's hitbox and uses a floatier jump (lower gravity, same height). Easy's windows are now 368–530 ms, and Normal's are 288–463 ms. The suite requires at least 350 ms on Easy and 250 ms on Normal.
Tiny hops on a quick tap
Fixed
The first version had "hold to jump higher". A fast tap on a phone (about 50 ms) peaked around 65 px, which barely clears a 64 px tombstone, so it would feel random to a small child.
Fixed: every jump is a full jump, and height comes from the double jump. There's a test that releasing straight away still gives a full-speed jump.
Spacing between obstacles
Confirmed clean
In a seeded 2-minute run of each mode, every gap between consecutive obstacles was checked against a full jump's travel plus the mode's reaction time (0.8 s on Easy, 0.45 s on Normal), measured at the speed the next obstacle arrives at. There were 0 violations. Easy never spawns the ghost or bats, and Normal spawns all 5 kinds.

03Accessibility & layout

Menus overflowed the game area
Fixed
A 1100×820 Chromium screenshot showed the "Start running!" button cut off below the 16:9 game area. On a 390 px phone the game area is about 200 px tall, so no overlay could fit. On an 844×390 landscape phone, the game area itself was taller than the screen.
Fixed:
  • The desktop card is tighter, with one row of costumes and the two modes side by side.
  • Under 720 px wide or 560 px tall, the menus sit under the game instead of over it.
  • The game area is capped by the viewport height, and the title is hidden on short screens.
  • Starting a run scrolls the game fully into view.
Re-checked at 1100×820, 1366×680, 390×844 and 844×390: the Start button is inside the panel, the whole game area is on screen while playing, the touch JUMP button is visible, and there's no horizontal scroll.
Contrast, keyboard and motion
Fixed
Computed WCAG ratios: body text 17.46, dim text 10.57 on the page and 10.77 on the card, the accent 10.26, button text 10.53, dim text on a selected choice 8.47, and the unlock line 11.58. Space, ↑ and W jump (with preventDefault, so the page doesn't scroll), P and Escape pause, and M mutes. The game pauses on a tab switch or window blur. The game area is focusable, and its label explains the controls.
Fixed: the hurt blink toggled 8 times a second. A 64 px sprite is far below WCAG 2.3.1's flash-area threshold, but the blink is now 2 Hz anyway, and just a steady fade under prefers-reduced-motion.
Real touch play not tried on a device
Open
Chromium's mobile emulation confirmed the layouts and that the JUMP button appears for coarse pointers. It can't tell whether tapping feels responsive on a real phone or tablet, or how loud the synth sounds on real speakers. Someone needs to play it once on a real device after deploy.
