Review #33 — sentinel-web.html device swarm

An update to Sentinel Web. While scanning, the Nearby screen now opens with a graphic based on the 3JS gallery's "3.3 Particle Swarm Core" (threejsGallery.html). The gallery's breathing particles form a core that stands for the user, and every device heard in the last minute orbits it as a brighter particle. A stronger signal orbits closer in, trackers and flagged devices have their own colours, and tapping a particle opens that device. The device list stays below it.

01Security

No three.js, no CDN, no CSP change
Confirmed clean

The gallery draws its swarm with three.js r128 from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Sentinel Web holds a location history, so the swarm doesn't load it. It reuses the gallery's orbit maths (radius, inclination, speed, the 1 + sin(1.1t + phase) · 0.16 breathing, an even spread over the sphere, slow spin) and draws it on a 2D canvas with 'lighter' compositing in place of additive blending. The CSP is byte-for-byte the one reviewed in 32.html, and script[src] is still 0 (the suite asserts both).

Advertised names reach the canvas only as drawn text
Confirmed clean

Tracker and flagged particles are labelled with the nickname, advertised name or family, through ctx.fillText. That paints pixels and can't be parsed as markup. Labels are cut to 22 characters. The canvas's accessible label is set with setAttribute and contains only counts. The source grep for innerHTML, insertAdjacentHTML, document.write and eval still finds nothing.

Tapping a particle only opens a device page
Confirmed clean

A tap finds the nearest drawn particle within 28 px and calls the existing openDetail() with that device's key. Particles for devices not yet linked (before the first 30-second cycle) have no key and can't be opened. Ignored devices aren't drawn. In Chromium, tapping the flagged tag's particle opened "Apple Find My". The finder rules are unchanged: "Find it" is still only on an active flag's page.

02Code quality & accessibility

Runs only while it can be seen
Confirmed clean

The animation runs only while scanning, on the Nearby screen, with the page visible and the canvas in the document. Each frame checks this and stops when any of it is false. In Chromium the animation stopped on leaving Nearby and when the page was hidden (raf 0 both times), and ran at 61 fps with 9 devices at 800 px and at 390 px. The canvas is one long-lived element moved back in on each re-render, so the swarm doesn't restart every 2 seconds (asserted). Its bitmap is capped at 2× device pixels. Background particles were cut from the gallery's 2,000 to 900, because this runs next to a live Bluetooth scan.

Contrast, motion and an accessible alternative
Confirmed clean

The swarm card is dark in both themes, because additive glow doesn't read on white. Measured against its #070a10 background: particle labels #dfe4ee at 15.5:1, legend text #b4bccb at 10.4:1, and particle colours (non-text, 3:1 needed) blue #5aa9ff at 8.1:1, orange #ffa94d at 10.4:1, red #ff5c5c at 6.5:1, gold #facc15 at 12.9:1. The ring labels are decorative gold at 60% opacity. Their bands are repeated in words in the legend, and each device's dBm is in the list below. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the swarm moves at 0.15× speed, the same factor the gallery uses (checked in Chromium, no errors). The canvas is role="img", and its label counts devices, trackers and flagged ones. The list below stays the full, keyboard-reachable version.

Tests
Confirmed clean

The sentinel-web suite gained 18 checks (89 passed, 0 failed). They cover: no swarm when not scanning, the swarm at the top of Nearby when scanning, the same element kept across renders, the accessible label's exact wording, the legend, device kinds (flagged vs plain), the orbit radius worked out by hand (0.55 at -45 dBm, 1.8 at -95 dBm, 1.175 at -70 dBm, clamped outside), stable per-device orbits, you at the canvas centre, and the outer orbit inside the padding.
