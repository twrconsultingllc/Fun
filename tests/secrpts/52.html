The memory is per device and per browser
Open

The name, the learned phrases and the skin are kept in each browser on each device. They don't follow you from phone to PC, and a private window or clearing site data wipes them. Syncing between devices would need an account or a server, which this page deliberately doesn't have. Recorded as a known limit, not a bug.
