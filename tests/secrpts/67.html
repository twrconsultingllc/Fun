01Security and privacy

Nothing new is stored
Confirmed clean

Which stone is open, whether the chat is open and how the caption is set are kept in memory for the visit only. None of them goes through store() or save(). The suite's one-place-that-saves checks pass unchanged: no localStorage write anywhere in the page, and exactly one 'readwrite' transaction, in flush().

No markup from text: the gauntlet is built with DOM calls
Confirmed clean

The gauntlet's card, tabs and picture are static markup in the page. Its links are made with createElement and textContent from the fixed GAUNTLET list, never from anything typed. The page still has no innerHTML (a suite check). The picture is an inline SVG with no script, no external reference and no event attribute. The stone colours come from CSS, not from attributes.

Links still do only what they say
Confirmed clean

Every gauntlet link is one of the help answer's own ⟦label|command⟧ pairs, so it is in CHIP_CMDS. The suite clicks through all six stones and checks every link against chipCmds(). The CAPABILITIES/NOT_COUNTED check ("every command link belongs to a capability") and "every link command is understood" both pass. The new link, "what can you do", was added to the help capability.

CSP unchanged
Confirmed clean

No new host, and no change to the CSP meta tag (the suite pins it). In Chromium, step 10 logged no CSP violation and no page error at any of its three sizes.

"See what I can do" taught him that a phrase meant help
Fixed

Found while building. After "I didn't understand", whatever you say next is taken as what you meant, and he learns it. Tapping the new "see what I can do" link would have taught him that "blorp the snorkel" means help.

Fix: The help answer no longer counts as what you meant: the gauntlet opens, and he still waits for your meaning. Two checks pin this. One confirms the phrase isn't taught. The other confirms that what you say next is still learned. A copy of the page without the fix failed both.
A popup you can leave, and use from a keyboard
Confirmed clean

The gauntlet is a role="dialog" with aria-modal, and its stones are a tablist. The arrow keys move between stones, and Tab stays inside it. Escape, CLOSE and a tap outside the card each close it. Focus goes back where it was, but never into the text box, where it would pop a phone's keyboard up. Every link is a real button at least 30 px tall (measured in Chromium).

Contrast
Confirmed clean

Worked out, not eyeballed. The six stone colours on each skin's background score at least 7.93:1, and the label colour at least 6.58:1 (Jarvis). The chat handle's label on each skin's gradient scores at least 6.07:1. All pass WCAG AA, which needs 4.5:1.

02Code quality and layout

The face sat under the chat on a phone
Fixed

On a phone, the skin, voice and HEY buttons wrap onto three rows, which leaves the face's area short. The face was drawn at min(78vw, 52vh, 420px) regardless, so it spilled down behind the chat, which took a fixed 26% of the screen.

Fix: The chat is a pull-up strip, as you chose (option D). Only his latest reply shows, scrolling inside the strip if it's long, at most a third of the screen. The face is sized by the space actually left (container-type:size, min(78vw, 96cqh, 420px)). The handle ("⌃ 6 EARLIER MESSAGES") opens the whole conversation in the face's place, by a tap, a swipe up or Enter, and a swipe down brings the face back. Measured in Chromium at 1280×800, 390×844 and 360×640: the face's bottom edge is above the strip, and the face is at least 140 px tall, after a long fact answer.
The help answer was one long paragraph
Fixed

62 links and about 3,500 characters, all spoken.

Fix: "Help" or "what can you do" opens the Infinity Gauntlet with stone tabs (option 3). Space is the projector, Mind is facts and his brains, Reality is quick answers, Power is protocols, Time is follow-ups and memory, and Soul is you and him. Each stone lists its commands, one per line, plus a short note that keeps the old paragraph's other information (dreaming, the 7-day rule, my dog is Rex, WebGPU). He says only "Here's what I can do. Pick a stone.", and each stone's name and theme when you tap it, through say() with a new quiet option that adds nothing to the chat. The full text still sits in the help answer between two invisible markers, so every existing check of what help mentions still reads it. Your favourites and the clearance extras stay in the chat line as links.
"Didn't understand" listed commands too
Fixed

The fallback answer carried its own list of 19 links.

Fix: It now says "Tap see what I can do for everything I know." with that one link, which opens the gauntlet, as you chose. Your favourites still lead it.
The projector caption covered the bottom of every scene
Fixed

The audit screenshot at 390×844 showed the memory core's lower stars and labels, Peru on the globe, the threat scan's target and the settings panel's note all under a caption of up to nine lines. The settings note overlapped the caption.

Fix: The caption is now a two-line peek. ⌃ MORE shows all of it (scrolling past 42% of the screen), ▾ HIDE tucks it to a small SHOW CAPTION button, and a swipe up or down does the same. Each new caption starts as a peek. The suit and memory labels, and the scan, now lay out above the caption's box. Measured in Chromium at all three sizes: the peek is at most 110 px tall, the tucked button at most 40 px, the memory core's labels don't overlap and stay between the top HUD and the caption, and the settings code stays clear of it.
Scenes centred in the space that's left
Fixed

Scenes were framed for the whole screen, so their lower part sat behind the caption and the text box.

Fix: Each frame, the projector centres the 3D view in the space between the top HUD and the caption, with setViewOffset, easing as the caption grows or hides. Tapping, picking and the labels all use the same projection, so they still line up. The suit schematic's own fixed 60 px nudge, which did the same job by hand, was removed so the figure isn't moved twice.
The projector title showed only "HOLO-PROJECTOR //" on a phone
Fixed

The scene's name was cut off by the HANDS and CLOSE buttons.

Fix: On screens under 600 px wide, the part before "//" is hidden, so the title reads "MEMORY CORE" or "EARTH". The title's text is unchanged, for anything that reads it. A long hint shows two lines on a phone and opens fully when tapped. Checked in Chromium: no "HOLO-PROJECTOR" and no cut-off at 390 and 360 wide.
