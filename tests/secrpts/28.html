Review #28 — daily-wire.html SW FL category

A new first category, SW FL, for Southwest Florida news centered on Naples and Bonita Springs and covering all of Collier and Lee counties. It draws on 13 feeds: four Google News searches, NWS alerts for both counties, and eight local outlets and city feeds. To support the searches, the fetcher gains two per-feed options: sourceFromItem, which shows the real publisher, and maxItems.

01Security

No new outside origins on the page
Confirmed clean
The page's CSP is unchanged: connect-src 'self', and img-src still lists the same 21 hosts. The new category adds no image hosts, because the Google News feeds are set to noImages and the local outlets' picture hosts are left for the next run to report in imageHostsDropped. Every card still goes through the page's own validation: http(s) links only, textContent only, and a known category id. The only new markup is two CSS color tokens and one class.
Google News links tell Google which stories are opened
Open
It's an accepted trade-off, and the user chose it knowing this. Google News search feeds link to news.google.com/rss/articles/…, which redirects to the publisher, so Google sees each click on those cards. rel="noopener noreferrer" and no-referrer still apply, so Google doesn't learn which page the click came from. These feeds are the only practical source for the Gannett papers (Naples Daily News, The News-Press, Marco Eagle), whose own feeds are unreliable. The page only renders what Google returns as plain text, the same as any other feed.
Aggregator publisher names are treated as untrusted text
Confirmed clean
sourceFromItem takes each card's source from the item's <source> element. It goes through the same toPlainText() tag scanner as titles, is capped at 80 characters, and the page caps it at 80 again and sets it with textContent. The " - Publisher" suffix is removed only when the title ends with exactly that publisher, so an earlier " - " in a headline stays. Both behaviors are tested.

02Correctness & accessibility

13 new feeds, most not yet confirmed
Open
The four Google News searches (Naples; Bonita Springs & Estero; Collier County with Marco Island, Immokalee and Golden Gate Estates; Lee County with Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Sanibel and Lehigh Acres) all end in when:7d, and each keeps up to 10 items. The NWS feed uses county zones FLC021 (Collier) and FLC071 (Lee). The eight direct feeds are the ones most likely to be dead or moved: WINK, Fox 4, WGCU, Gulfshore Business, Coastal Breeze News, the Naples Daily News and News-Press legacy Gannett feeds, and the City of Naples news flash. None of them could be fetched from the claude.ai container. The next workflow run reports each one in the footer's Feed status, and dead ones get removed in a follow-up.
Colour contrast for the new category
Confirmed clean
Computed WCAG ratios for --swfl, light/dark: the category label on a card is 5.65/8.92; the large source name on a no-picture card is 4.62/6.75, against a 3:1 large-text minimum; muted text on that panel is 5.08/5.47. Headless Chromium screenshots with the SW FL filter on, at 1280 px light and 390 px dark, showed no page errors and no horizontal scroll.
