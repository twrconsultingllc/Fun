Open

A count lost to an instant reload
Fixed

The Chromium check of saves surviving a reload ("today's counts") lost the galaxy's usage count in 2 of 4 runs, yesterday and today. The step reloads the page within milliseconds of the last command. A usage count goes to IndexedDB in a transaction that finishes a moment later, and a reload in that moment cancels it. On a real device, that's at most one count lost by reloading instantly after a command. It has been the same since Session 9, so it's noted here rather than worked around in the page.

Fix: The step waits half a second before reloading, with a comment saying why. It's the step that was wrong: it means to check saves surviving a reload, not that race.
